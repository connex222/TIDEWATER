How to Play

Goal: earn 15 Renown (10 in Quick mode) to win the Admiralty Charter.

1 · Join the fleet

Enter the TV's four-letter code and your name, choose a unique captain, then tap I'm Ready.

2 · Claim two islands

Take turns placing two free settlements on production islands. Placement moves forward through the captains and then back in reverse (snake order). Each settlement starts with one Warship.

3 · Trade and expand

Earn Fish, Timber, Spice and Silk from your islands. Use resources to settle, improve harbours, chart sea lanes and strengthen your fleet. Low Tide closes one colour of lane each round; next round's tide is previewed.

4 · Plot two secret orders

Choose two orders on your phone each round. Hoist Sails to lock them. Everyone reveals in rotating order when all are ready or the timer expires. Use Hold Position to skip a slot. Failed contested actions earn 1 Gold.

5 · Win renown

Ports, Voyage Contracts, trading routes and fleet achievements earn Renown. Progress through Sail, Cannon and Steam.

Stories of the Isles

Every chart points towards the Grand Bazaar, a free port ruled by no captain. It is said that the first Admiralty Charter was sealed on a salt-stained scrap of sailcloth, after a storm left eight merchant houses trapped together in a sheltered bay.

Phase 11 scope: settlements, Ports, lanes, Warships, Market, Explore, Raid, contracts, private trading, era upgrades and trophies are playable. Advanced Weather and captain perks are available in Advanced mode. Naval battles resolve simultaneously at Reveal: each fleet can commit only once; defending captains win ties, and results appear in the battle report. Port upgrades add 2 Renown on top of the Harbour settlement’s 1. Optional local sound cues signal trades, incoming deals, battles and the finish. The host may Pause Voyage; after a restart your captain and private orders return with the match, and time remains frozen until the host resumes.
