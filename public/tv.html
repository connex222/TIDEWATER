How to Play Tidewater

Your mission: become the first trading house to reach 15 Renown (10 in Quick mode), or lead when the final round ends.

1 · Establish your house

Scan the code with your phone, select one of eight captains, and press Ready. When everyone is ready, the host presses Set Sail.

2 · Claim the islands

Each captain chooses two different unclaimed production islands, in snake order. Your new settlements each begin with one Warship. Your hold starts with 2 Gold and 1 Fish, Timber, Spice and Silk.

3 · Master wind, tide and trade

Every round your islands produce resources. Blue, Green or Silver shipping lanes close at Low Tide. Plan two orders secretly on your phone, then watch them resolve on the shared map. Markets react to everyone’s sales.

4 · Win the Admiralty Charter

Expand to new harbours, improve your ports, link trade routes, complete Voyage Contracts and command fleets. Sail gives way to Cannon, then Steam.

What is playable in Phase 7?

Room joining, chart generation, opening settlements, two private orders each round, buying and selling goods, settling islands and harbours, building Ports, charting lanes, launching Warships, timed reveals, rotated resolution and basic Renown are functional. Voyage Contracts, Explore, Raid, player trades, era upgrades and trophies are now implemented. Advanced Weather, captain perks and contract selection are included; Phase 6 adds simultaneous naval battles and battle reports. Phase 7 adds a nautical music loop and richer sound effects, with separate controls.

Lore: On the edge of the chart lies the Grand Bazaar, where no flag may fly but every merchant can make a fortune.
