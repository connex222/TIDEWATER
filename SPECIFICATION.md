# Tidewater
### An island trading-kingdom game for the TV and phones

| | |
|---|---|
| **Players** | 2–8 (best with 4) |
| **Length** | Quick 30 min · Standard 45–60 min |
| **Age** | 10+ |
| **Setting** | An invented sun-soaked archipelago, Age of Sail to early Steam |
| **Inspired by** | Catan (resources) · Ticket to Ride (sea lanes) · Risk (fleets and ports) · Civilization (eras) |
| **Screens** | TV = shared sea chart · Phone/tablet = private captain's log |

---

## 1. Synopsis

The Tidewater Isles are rich and unclaimed. Up to eight merchant houses sail in to settle islands, chart sea lanes between ports, and fill their warehouses with fish, timber, spice and silk.

But the sea is alive. Every round the **tide and wind change**, opening some lanes and closing others. Plans that worked last round may be blocked this round. Merchants plan in secret on their phones, then the TV plays the round out: ships glide across the chart, harbours grow into bustling ports, and rival fleets collide in cannon-smoke.

Over three eras — **Sail, Cannon and Steam** — the islands grow from fishing villages into trading empires. The first house to earn **15 Renown** wins the Admiralty Charter.

**The feeling:** bright, breezy and sociable. More trading and racing than fighting, with the tide adding a fun bit of "plans gone wrong".

**What makes it different from Iron Crown:** the **Tide Chart** changes the map every round, and a **Market Board** where goods prices rise and fall.

## 2. Game Bits (Components)

### 2.1 The Sea Chart
- **37 hex tiles**: islands and open sea, generated each game.

| Tile | Produces | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fishing Isle | Fish | 6 | Nets, little boats, gulls |
| Wooded Isle | Timber | 6 | Palms and hardwoods |
| Spice Isle | Spice | 5 | Terraces, red and yellow crops |
| Silk Isle | Silk | 5 | Mulberry groves, dye vats |
| Harbour Isle | Gold | 6 | Neutral harbour, starts unclaimed |
| Reef | Nothing | 4 | +1 defence to holder; ships go round slowly |
| Open Sea | Nothing | 4 | Can't be claimed, lanes cross it |
| The Grand Bazaar | Nothing | 1 | Centre island, can't be claimed; lanes touching it earn +1 Gold |

- **Sea Lanes:** 18 dotted shipping lanes between Harbour Isles and the Grand Bazaar, 2–5 segments long.
- **Lane colours:** each lane is one of **3 colours — Blue, Green, Silver** — which matters for the Tide (below).

### 2.2 The Tide Chart (new)
- A dial in the TV corner. Each round it spins to show which lane colour is **Low Tide**.
- **Low Tide lanes** can't be claimed this round and don't pay income.
- The **next round's tide is shown in advance** (as a smaller icon), so players can plan.

### 2.3 The Market Board (new)
- A strip on the TV showing the current **sell price in Gold** for each good (1–4).
- When anyone sells a good, its price **drops 1** (min 1). Unsold goods rise **1** each round (max 4).
- This replaces most bank trading and creates "sell before everyone else does" moments.

### 2.4 Goods

| Good | Icon idea | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Fish** | Silver fish | Settlements, crews |
| **Timber** | Plank stack | Settlements, ships, lanes |
| **Spice** | Pepper sack | Ports, research |
| **Silk** | Folded cloth | Ports, lanes |
| **Gold** | Doubloon | Crews, research, anything via the market |

### 2.5 Player Pieces

| Piece | What it is | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Settlement** | Marks an island you own | Stilt houses with a flag |
| **Port** | Upgraded Harbour | Stone quay, lighthouse, warehouses |
| **Lane** | Claimed sea lane | Dotted line becomes a solid wake in your colour |
| **Merchant Ship** | Animation only | Sails your lanes each round |
| **Warship** | Defence/attack strength | Little galleon with number on its sail |

### 2.6 Cards
- **Voyage Contracts (24)** — secret goals: "Link Coral Point to Saltmere" worth 2–4 Renown.
- **Era Upgrade Cards (9)**
- **Weather Cards (12)** — optional events.

### 2.7 Trophies
- **Longest Trade Route** (+2 Renown)
- **Mightiest Fleet** (+2 Renown)
- **Master Trader** — most Gold earned from the Market this era (+1 Renown, reset each era)

## 3. Setup (automatic)

1. TV shows room code and QR. Players join on phones and pick an avatar.
2. Host chooses mode.
3. TV generates the chart and sets all Market prices to **2**.
4. Each player gets **3 Voyage Contracts**, keeps at least 2.
5. Snake-order: each player places **2 Settlements** on free production islands, each with **1 Warship**.
6. Each player starts with **2 Gold** and **1 of each good**.

---

## 4. How a Round Works

### Phase 1 — Tide & Income (~10 seconds)
- The Tide dial spins. One lane colour goes to Low Tide.
- Each owned island produces **1** of its good.
- Harbour **1 Gold**, Port **2 Gold**.
- Each owned lane **not** at Low Tide pays **1 Gold** (merchant ships sail along it).
- Last place on Renown gets **+1 Gold**.

### Phase 2 — Plan (simultaneous, 60–90 seconds)
Each player secretly chooses **2 orders** and taps **Hoist Sails**.

### Phase 3 — Reveal (~30–60 seconds)
- Orders resolve in rotating turn order.
- Failed orders refund and give **+1 Gold**.
- **Sea battles** resolve together at the end.
- **Market prices** update at the very end.

---

## 5. The Orders

| Order | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Settle** | 1 Timber + 1 Fish | Claim a free island next to an owned island or owned lane. |
| **Build Port** | 2 Spice + 2 Silk + 1 Timber | Turn an owned Harbour into a Port. |
| **Chart Lane** | 1 Timber + 1 Silk **per segment** | Claim a whole lane (not one at Low Tide). One end must touch your island. |
| **Launch Warships** | 2 Gold each (max 3) | Place on owned islands. |
| **Go to Market** | Free | Sell any goods at current prices **or** buy any good at price +1. |
| **Offer a Deal** | Free | Propose a swap to one player (20 seconds to accept). |
| **Explore** | 3 Gold + 1 Spice | Draw a new Voyage Contract **or** buy a leftover Upgrade. Once per round. |
| **Raid** | Free (uses Warships) | Attack an enemy island next to yours, sending Warships from adjacent islands. |

### Sea Battles
- **Strength** = Warships + bonuses + **one die**.
- **Defender bonuses:** Reef +1, Port +1.
- Ties to defender. Loser loses **half (rounded up)**, winner loses **1**.
- No elimination: a house with no islands re-settles 1 island free next round.

## 6. Eras

| Era | Rounds | Chart look |
|---|---|---|
| **Sail** | 1–4 | Dawn, calm turquoise water, small sloops |
| **Cannon** | 5–8 | Bright noon, bigger galleons, forts on Ports |
| **Steam** | 9–12 | Sunset, paddle steamers with smoke, lanterns on quays |

| Era | Upgrade | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sail | **Shipwrights** | Lanes cost 1 less Timber per lane. |
| Sail | **Fishmongers' Guild** | Fish always sells for at least 3. |
| Sail | **Privateers** | Gain 2 Warships now and 1 free each era. |
| Cannon | **Deep Harbours** | Ports cost 1 less Silk. |
| Cannon | **Trade Winds** | Your lanes pay even at Low Tide. |
| Cannon | **Coastal Forts** | All your islands get +1 defence. |
| Steam | **Steam Packets** | You may Chart Lanes at Low Tide. |
| Steam | **Admiralty House** | Your first Port becomes Admiralty House: +2 Renown. |
| Steam | **Chamber of Commerce** | Each completed Voyage Contract +1 Renown. |

---

## 7. Renown and Winning

| Source | Renown |
|---|---|
| Settlement on a Harbour | 1 |
| Port | 2 |
| Completed Voyage Contract | 2–4 |
| Longest Trade Route | 2 |
| Mightiest Fleet (min 5 Warships) | 2 |
| Master Trader (per era) | 1 |
| Admiralty House | 2 |

- First to **15 Renown** after a Reveal wins (10 in Quick).
- Otherwise highest after round 12. Tie-break: Gold, then lanes.

## 11. UI Design

### 11.1 Visual style
- **Theme:** antique sea chart come to life — painted islands on glowing turquoise water, compass roses, sea-serpent doodles in the margins.
- **Board:** top-down with a slight tilt, animated water shimmer.

### 11.2 Colour palette
| Role | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Chart paper `#F4ECD8` | Night sea `#0D1B26` |
| Water | Turquoise `#3FB8C4` | Deep teal `#16505A` |
| Panel | Driftwood `#6B4E35` | Ship's hull `#2B1F15` |
| Accent | Brass `#C9A227` | Lantern gold `#F0C24B` |
| Text main | `#1B1A17` | `#F3EAD7` |
| Text muted | `#5A5246` | `#AFA48F` |
| Good | `#2E7D32` | `#5CC163` |
| Bad | `#B3261E` | `#FF6B5E` |

Lane colours must also have a **pattern** (Blue = solid, Green = dashed, Silver = dotted) so colour-blind players can tell them apart.

### 11.3 Fonts (bundled woff2)
- **Headings:** *IM Fell English* (old chart lettering).
- **Body/numbers:** *Inter*.
